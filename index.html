HOW TO PLAY

GOAL
Go deeper into Hell and survive. Defeat waves of demons. If your whole team dies at the same time → you restart.

CONTROLS (Phone)
• Left stick = Move
• 👊 = Punch / Basic attack
• 🔥 = Skill 1   💥 = Skill 2   ⚡ = Ultimate
• ⬆ = Jump
• Rotate phone to landscape (required)

CONTROLS (Computer)
• WASD or Arrows = Move
• 1 = Punch   2 / 3 / 4 = Skills & Ultimate
• Space = Jump

FIGHTING (not free)
Every power costs Mana (blue bar).
Basic attack is almost free. Skills and Ultimate cost more.
Mana slowly refills. Don’t spam Ultimates or you will run out.

TEAM RULES
• Each player must choose a different magic
• One player should be the Healer (checkbox)
• Stay close to teammates → your powers get stronger (synergy)
• You can see each other’s blocky characters + name tags live

GET STRONGER (Competitive)
• Kill enemies and deal damage → earn XP
• Level up = more HP, Mana, Speed, Damage
• Live leaderboard shows Kills / Damage / Level
• Compete with friends for top rank!

HOW TO BEAT IT
1. Pick different magics + one Healer
2. Move together, don’t split too far
3. Kill small demons first, then elites
4. When an enemy summons, focus the summoner
5. Heal when someone is low
6. Survive longer = go deeper = win

GET STRONGER & COMPETE
Kill enemies → gain XP → Level Up.
Each level = more HP, Mana, Speed, and Damage.
Scoreboard shows who has most Kills and Damage. Friendly competition!

Tip: Lightning and Wind are fast. Earth is tanky. Water heals. Anti-Magic is strong against magic enemies.
